# Sales Playbook

## Sales objective

Win the first real paying customer without fake testimonials, fake transactions, or ad spend.

## Positioning

UltraLaunch AI is not positioned as a generic copy generator. It is an **AI launch operator** that gives a founder or agency the complete first-customer sales motion from one brief.

## Offer ladder

1. **Free** — 3 launch kits/month, no card.
2. **Starter** — $19/month for founders validating one or two offers.
3. **Pro** — $49/month for agencies/operators running multiple launch kits.
4. **Business** — $149/month for teams with higher usage and priority support.
5. **Guided pilot** — $199 one-time implementation sprint to close the first paid customer faster.

## Discovery script

```text
Thanks for taking the time. I want to understand whether UltraLaunch AI can help you get a real customer conversation faster.

1. What are you trying to sell in the next 30 days?
2. Who is the exact buyer?
3. What pain or trigger makes them care now?
4. What have you tried so far?
5. What price would make this worth selling?
6. If we had the landing copy and outreach today, who would you send it to first?
```

## Demo flow

1. Show the homepage promise.
2. Register/login.
3. Generate a kit from the prospect's real offer.
4. Show:
   - ICP and pains
   - hero headline
   - pricing/pilot recommendation
   - cold email/DM
   - objections
   - 24-hour plan
5. Ask: "Which section would save you the most time this week?"
6. Close with a guided pilot or subscription.

## Closing script

```text
Based on what you told me, the highest leverage next step is not more product work. It is a sharper paid pilot and a first outreach batch.

I can refine this into a complete launch kit and help you send the first 25 targeted messages. The guided pilot is $199, and if you continue with UltraLaunch AI I credit that toward the subscription.

Do you want to start with the pilot today?
```

## Objection handling

### "I can just use ChatGPT."

```text
Absolutely — if you already know the exact prompts and structure. UltraLaunch AI saves the workflow: buyer, pain, pricing, landing page, outreach, content, objections, and a 24-hour action plan in one place, with account history, usage limits, support, and billing.
```

### "We are not ready to launch."

```text
That is exactly when this helps. The kit shows what is unclear before you spend more time building. You can validate the offer with a paid pilot first.
```

### "No budget."

```text
Start with the free kit. If it creates a useful customer conversation, upgrade or use the guided pilot. I do not want you paying before there is a credible path to use it.
```

### "Will this guarantee customers?"

```text
No. It guarantees speed and completeness of launch assets, not market demand or revenue. The goal is to get you into real conversations faster and improve from real feedback.
```

## Manual outreach quota for launch day

- 100 leads identified
- 50 personalized DMs sent
- 25 emails sent where appropriate
- 10 follow-ups scheduled
- 5 demos offered
- 1–3 paid pilot asks

## Success metric for first 24 hours

Primary: one real paid pilot or subscription.

Secondary:

- 5+ qualified replies
- 2+ demos booked
- 10+ signups
- 5+ launch kits generated
- clear objections to improve landing page
