# Customer Acquisition Strategy

## Ideal Customer Profile

### Primary ICP

**AI agency owner or solo founder launching offers repeatedly.**

Traits:

- Sells services, SaaS, templates, or ecommerce improvements.
- Needs speed more than perfect customization.
- Can pay $19–$149/month or $199 for a guided pilot.
- Has authority to buy without procurement.
- Active on LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, founder communities, or Shopify/ecommerce communities.

### Secondary ICP

**Consultant/productized service operator.**

Pain:

- Knows their expertise but struggles to package it into a clear paid offer.
- Needs landing copy, pricing, objections, sales scripts, and content quickly.

### Tertiary ICP

**E-commerce operator testing a new product/category.**

Pain:

- Needs product descriptions, SEO briefs, competitor angles, retention emails, and customer support macros.

## Lead list strategy

Build a lead sheet with these columns:

- Name
- Company
- Role
- URL/profile
- Trigger observed
- Likely offer
- Personalization note
- Channel
- Status
- Reply
- Next action

### Best trigger events

- Posted about launching an MVP.
- Asked for feedback on a landing page.
- Announced an AI agency/service.
- Hiring for growth/marketing but still early.
- E-commerce founder discussing conversion or retention.
- Founder has a product but unclear positioning/pricing.

### Sources

- LinkedIn keyword searches: "launching my SaaS", "AI agency", "productized service", "Shopify growth".
- X/Twitter search: "building in public", "landing page feedback", "first customer".
- Product Hunt upcoming/recent launches.
- Indie Hackers product updates.
- Shopify partner/agency directories.
- Founder Slack/Discord communities where outreach is allowed.

## Outreach strategy

Principles:

- Do not spam.
- Personalize the first line.
- Offer a concrete sample launch kit, not a generic demo.
- Sell a low-friction paid pilot before pushing annual subscriptions.
- Never claim fake customer results.

### LinkedIn/X DM

```text
Hey {{first_name}} — saw your post about {{specific_trigger}}.

I’m launching UltraLaunch AI: it turns a rough offer into a launch-ready landing page story, pricing ladder, outreach scripts, content ideas, objections, and a 24-hour action plan.

If you send me one offer you’re testing, I’ll generate a sample kit and walk you through it in 15 minutes. Useful if you’re trying to get real customer conversations before building more.

Want me to make one for {{company/product}}?
```

### Cold email

Subject options:

- `Launch kit idea for {{company}}`
- `Can I make a 24-hour offer kit for you?`
- `Saw {{trigger}} — quick launch asset?`

```text
Hi {{first_name}},

I noticed {{specific_trigger}} and thought {{company}} might be testing a new offer or positioning.

I built UltraLaunch AI, a launch workspace that generates the ICP, positioning, pricing, landing-page copy, email/DM scripts, short-form content, SEO briefs, objections, and 24-hour execution plan from one brief.

If you send one current offer, I can generate a sample kit and review it with you in 15 minutes. If it is useful, the guided pilot is $199 and can be credited toward a subscription.

Worth a look this week?

{{your_name}}
```

### Follow-up 1

```text
Quick follow-up — the value is not “more AI copy.” It is getting the buyer, promise, price, landing copy, objections, and first outreach messages into one plan so you can test demand today.

Want me to generate one sample around {{company/product}}?
```

### Follow-up 2

```text
Closing the loop. If now is not the right time, no worries.

If useful, I can send a free example launch kit structure you can reuse internally.
```

## Demo flow

1. Confirm the buyer and offer in 2 minutes.
2. Generate or open their launch kit.
3. Show these sections first:
   - ICP and pain
   - landing-page hero
   - pricing/paid pilot
   - outreach DM/email
   - objections
4. Ask: "Which part would you actually use today?"
5. Offer paid pilot:
   - "I can refine this into a complete kit and help you send the first 25 messages. Pilot is $199. Want to do it?"

## First customer offer

Use a services-assisted SaaS sale for speed:

- Free generated kit
- $199 guided implementation pilot
- Subscription upsell after pilot
- Credit pilot fee toward subscription if they continue

## Call-to-action

Primary CTA:

> Generate your free launch kit.

Sales CTA:

> Send me one offer. I’ll generate the kit and show you the fastest path to a paid pilot.
